# 8 High-End Yoga Retreat Website References

Researched: 8 October 2026.

A curated inspiration list for a premium yoga camp website. This combines dedicated yoga retreat centres with luxury wellness resorts that offer yoga: the former are useful for retreat structure and community, the latter for elevated hospitality presentation. The order is an editorial shortlist, not an objective ranking.

Official pages and yoga relevance were checked through web research. Design directions below are recommendations for what to study or adapt; this is not a browser-tested audit of animation, mobile layouts, or performance.

## 1. Santani Wellness Kandy

**Website:** [santani.com/santaniwellness-kandy](https://www.santani.com/santaniwellness-kandy/)

**Why it belongs:** A luxury wellness retreat whose story connects open pavilions, mountain nature, food, and restoration. The site includes a film entry point, yoga and meditation, the Surya Shala, and retreat-hosting information.

**Study:** How the destination and architecture create desire before the visitor explores individual programmes. Follow the journey from the homepage to accommodation and yoga facilities.

**Idea to adapt:** Open with a beautiful landscape film, then introduce the yoga space, daily rhythm, and rooms as chapters of one stay.

**Best fit:** A cinematic yoga escape with strong architecture and landscape assets.

## 2. COMO Shambhala Estate

**Website:** [comoshambhala.com/como-shambhala-estate](https://www.comoshambhala.com/como-shambhala-estate)

**Why it belongs:** A luxury retreat in a Balinese jungle setting, with wellness paths, expert practitioners, movement, and multi-night experiences presented within one coherent offering.

**Study:** The separation of experiences, experts, and practical information. Its [Connect to Rebalance programme](https://www.comoshambhala.com/como-shambhala-estate/experiences/wellness-paths/connect-to-rebalance) is especially relevant to yoga, meditation, and breathwork.

**Idea to adapt:** Pair a restrained editorial direction with clearly named retreat packages and concise inclusions. Let the setting and teaching expertise carry the premium positioning.

**Best fit:** Understated, sophisticated luxury.

## 3. Six Senses Vana

**Website:** [Six Senses Vana](https://www.sixsenses.com/en/hotels-resorts/asia-the-pacific/india/vana/)

**Why it belongs:** A forest-oriented wellness retreat combining yoga, Ayurveda, and other wellness traditions. Its introduction connects the emotional promise of the stay with concrete inclusions such as meals, daily activities, and consultations.

**Study:** The film-led introduction, nature-based narrative, and explanation of what a retreat stay actually includes.

**Idea to adapt:** After an atmospheric opening, answer the practical question immediately: what does a guest experience each day, and what is included?

**Best fit:** A premium retreat with a carefully guided guest journey.

## 4. Euphoria Retreat

**Website:** [euphoriaretreat.com](https://www.euphoriaretreat.com/)

**Why it belongs:** A Greek wellness destination with a distinctive Byzantine-inspired architectural identity and a substantial philosophy behind its programmes.

**Study:** How architecture, place, philosophy, and programme discovery work together to establish a recognisable identity. This is a broader wellness reference rather than a dedicated yoga camp.

**Idea to adapt:** Build a visual identity around one memorable feature of the property: a domed shala, courtyard, stone arch, or forest pavilion.

**Best fit:** A dramatic, architectural interpretation of luxury wellness.

## 5. Ananda in the Himalayas

**Website:** [anandaspa.com](https://www.anandaspa.com/)

**Why it belongs:** A luxury wellness retreat in the Himalayan foothills with yoga and meditation as substantial parts of its offering, including dedicated yoga-related programmes.

**Study:** How the site establishes expertise, explains its traditions, and organises an extensive [programme collection](https://www.anandaspa.com/en/programmes).

**Idea to adapt:** Give teachers, teaching approach, retreat suitability, and programme details enough prominence to support the beautiful destination story.

**Best fit:** Heritage luxury with a serious yoga practice.

## 6. Soulshine Bali

**Website:** [soulshinebali.com](https://www.soulshinebali.com/)

**Why it belongs:** A barefoot-luxury resort that combines yoga, music, food, and community. Its offer includes retreat packages, yoga teacher training, and hosting your own retreat.

**Study:** The expressive brand voice, room and package presentation, and separate journeys for retreat guests and retreat organisers.

**Idea to adapt:** Show the human side of camp: communal meals, movement, music, friendships, and the people behind the experience. Make the programme feel enjoyable as well as restorative.

**Best fit:** A fashionable, social, energetic yoga retreat brand.

## 7. Sen Wellness Sanctuary

**Website:** [senwellnesssanctuary.com](https://www.senwellnesssanctuary.com/)

**Why it belongs:** A Sri Lankan sanctuary focused on yoga and Ayurveda, with personalised retreats and a daily rhythm that includes practice, meditation, and restorative activities.

**Study:** The [bespoke retreat presentation](https://www.senwellnesssanctuary.com/bespoke-retreats), sample daily rhythm, and personal enquiry journey.

**Idea to adapt:** Include a beautifully paced “a day at camp” section, followed by a short enquiry form that helps guests find the right retreat.

**Best fit:** An intimate, personal, nature-connected experience.

## 8. Suryalila

**Website:** [suryalila.com](https://www.suryalila.com/)

**Why it belongs:** The most directly relevant dedicated yoga-centre reference in this list: retreats, teacher training, personalised stays, organic vegetarian food, and a permaculture connection.

**Study:** How it separates upcoming retreats, flexible stays, and teacher training so different visitors can find their next step.

**Idea to adapt:** Use clear retreat cards with dates, teacher, yoga style, duration, and availability. Pair this practical structure with a more luxurious visual direction from the first five references.

**Best fit:** A real yoga camp with a recurring retreat calendar. Its value here is authentic yoga depth and useful structure rather than ultra-luxury hotel positioning.

## Strongest direction for a new yoga camp website

Start with **Santani, COMO Shambhala, and Soulshine** for three distinct creative directions: cinematic nature, restrained luxury, and expressive community.

For the eventual website, combine:

- **Atmosphere:** landscape, architecture, and real practice photography.
- **Story:** a clear promise, the place, the teachers, and a typical day.
- **Booking clarity:** dates, duration, inclusions, accommodation, and one obvious enquiry or booking action.
- **Camp relevance:** borrow retreat-calendar structure from Suryalila and daily-rhythm storytelling from Sen.

Use these as inspiration for an original design; keep brand assets, photography, and copy specific to the new camp.
